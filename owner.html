Quick guide

  • New product: open the editor, click “Products”, then “Product Catalog”, then “Add products”.
  • Photo: inside a product, use the Photo field to upload an image from your device.
  • Stock: change the “Stock quantity” number. At 0 the shop shows “Sold out”.
  • Hide without deleting: untick “Active”.
  • Save: click “Publish” at the top of the editor. Allow about a minute for the site to update.
  • Customer messages: in your Netlify dashboard, open the “Forms” tab.
